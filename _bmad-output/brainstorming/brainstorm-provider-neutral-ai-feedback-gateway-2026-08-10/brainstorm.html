A provider-neutral feedback boundary that stays useful when the provider, quota, licence, or cost is uncertain.

The invariant

unknown→explicit state→safe projection→honest learner action

Never turn unavailable into success, zero, a fake task, a fake cost, or an official score. The same rule ties together licensing, retries, observability, quota, and UX.

Ideas that survived the pressure test

Policy as dataVersion quota, prompt provenance, response schema, and cost buckets. Controllers never own limits.
Idempotency firstReserve quota and record usage by owner plus request key; detect conflicting retries by fingerprint.
Firewall before logsAllowlist the learner projection before response or observability emission. Logs are a boundary too.
Advisory onlyMake feedback immutable and structurally unable to mutate progress, official score, or hidden rubric state.
Fail closedWithdrawn source, malformed output, unknown cost, or unavailable provider becomes a named outcome.
